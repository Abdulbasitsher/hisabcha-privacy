🗑️ Data Control

Delete Your Account & Data

You have full control over your data. Submit a deletion request anytime — no need to reinstall the app.

Google Play Compliant · Responds within 48 hours
ℹ️
Two options available: Request full account deletion (removes everything), or delete only specific data — such as sales history or product records — while keeping your account active.
📧

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email to our support team. Please follow these steps so we can verify and process your request quickly:

1
Email from your registered address

Send the request from the email linked to your Hisabcha account so we can verify your identity.

2
Use the subject: "Account Deletion Request"

This routes your request to the right team immediately.

3
Specify what you want deleted

Full account + all data, or only specific data such as sales history or product list.

4
We confirm and process within 30 days

You receive a confirmation when we receive your request, and again when deletion is complete.

✉️ Email hisabcha@gmail.com
📋

What Data Gets Deleted

Here is exactly what happens to each type of data when your deletion request is processed:

Data Type Action Timeframe
Account infoName, email, password Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Product & inventory dataItems, quantities, prices, barcodes Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Sales & transaction historyPOS records, receipts Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Cloud backup dataAny synced data on our servers Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Crash & diagnostic logsAnonymous device/error logs Retained briefly Auto-purged within 90 days
⚠️
Note on retained data: Anonymous crash logs contain no personally identifiable information. They are retained up to 90 days solely for security and fraud prevention, then permanently deleted. This is disclosed per Google Play policy.

Delete Only Specific Data (Keep Your Account)

You don't have to delete your account entirely. You can request deletion of specific data only. The following can be deleted individually:

  • Sales and transaction history
  • Product and inventory records
  • Barcode / SKU data
  • Cloud backup data

To request partial data deletion, email hisabcha@gmail.com and specify which data you want removed.
