Overview

Hisabcha ("we", "our", or "us") is a point-of-sale and inventory management application designed for shopkeepers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — including your right to delete your account and data at any time.

📦

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our services:

  • Account data: Name, email address, and password used to create your account.
  • Business data: Product names, inventory counts, prices, barcodes, and sales records you enter.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers used for app functionality and crash reporting.
  • Camera access: Used solely for barcode and QR code scanning. No images or video are stored or transmitted.
📷

Camera Permission

Hisabcha requests camera access for one purpose only:

📷
Barcode & QR Code Scanning — The camera activates only when you choose to scan a product barcode or SKU. No photos or video recordings are taken, saved, or uploaded at any time.

You may deny camera permission at any time in device settings. Barcode scanning will be unavailable but all other features will work normally.

⚙️

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Hisabcha account.
  • To provide and improve inventory and POS features.
  • To save your business data locally on your device.
  • To sync data to secure cloud storage if you enable backup (optional).
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues.
🤝

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or business information to third parties and do not share it with advertisers. Data may be shared only in limited cases:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g. crash analytics), under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
🔐

Data Storage & Security

Your inventory and sales data is stored locally on your device by default. Optional cloud backup uses industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

🗑️

Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. When we receive a verified deletion request:

  • Your account is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • All personal data (name, email, password) is removed from our servers.
  • All business data (products, sales, inventory) is permanently erased.
  • Anonymous diagnostic logs are retained up to 90 days for fraud prevention, then fully purged.
To submit a request, visit our or email support@hisabcha.com.
👤

Children's Privacy

Hisabcha is intended for use by adults (shopkeepers and business owners). We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

✅

Your Rights

  • Access the data stored by the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Request only partial data deletion while keeping your account active.
  • Revoke any device permission (camera, storage) via device settings.
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us.
🔄

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
