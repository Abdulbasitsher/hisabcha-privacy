🔒 Privacy Policy

Hisabcha – Smart Inventory & POS for Shopkeepers

Last updated: May 1, 2025

Overview

Hisabcha ("we", "our", or "us") is a point-of-sale and inventory management application designed for shopkeepers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Business data: Product names, inventory counts, prices, and sales records that you enter manually.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers for app functionality and crash reporting.
  • Camera access: Used solely for scanning barcodes and QR codes to add or look up products. No images or video are stored or transmitted.

Camera Permission

Hisabcha requests access to your device's camera for one purpose only:

📷 Barcode & QR Code Scanning — The camera is activated only when you choose to scan a product barcode or SKU. No photos or video recordings are taken, saved, or uploaded at any time.

You may deny camera permission at any time in your device settings. Barcode scanning will be unavailable, but all other app features will continue to work normally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the inventory and POS features of the app.
  • To save your business data locally on your device.
  • To sync data to secure cloud storage if you enable backup (optional).
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or business information to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers. Data may be shared only in the following limited cases:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., crash analytics), under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data Storage & Security

Your inventory and sales data is stored locally on your device by default. Optional cloud backup uses industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Children's Privacy

Hisabcha is intended for use by adults (shopkeepers and business owners). We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored by the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke any device permission (camera, storage) via your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
